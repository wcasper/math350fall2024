## Problem 1

Let $$(M,d)$$ be a metric space.
A subset $$S\subseteq M$$ is called **dense** if $$\overline{S} = M$$.

Prove that if $$M$$ has a finite, dense subset then $$M$$ itself must be a finite set.

## Problem 2

Consider the function $$f: [0,1]\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$$ defined by

$$f(x) = \left\lbrace\begin{array}{cc}
1, & x = 1/n\ \text{for some positive integer}\ n\\
0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{array}\right.$$

Determine (with proof) whether or not $$f$$ is Riemann integrable on the interval $$[0,1]$$.
